Privacy Policy — FreeOrNot (Paywall Detector)

Last updated: 23 August 2026

FreeOrNot is a Chrome extension that warns you if a website is likely to require payment before you can download or export your work. This page explains what data the extension collects and how it's used.

What we collect

When you visit a page and use the extension (via the toolbar icon, or automatically for badge status), we send the following to our backend server to classify the site:

  • The domain and URL of the page you're viewing
  • A short excerpt of visible page text (used to detect payment-related language, buttons, and pricing links)
  • Basic page signals (e.g. presence of file upload inputs, button labels like "Export"/"Upgrade")

We do not collect passwords, form field contents, payment details, or any personally identifiable information.

How we use it

This data is used solely to classify whether a site is Free, Limited Free, Likely Paid, or Paid Required, and to improve the accuracy of that classification over time. Classification results are cached by domain (not by individual user) so the same site doesn't need to be re-analyzed for every user.

What we store

Classification results (domain → verdict) are stored on our backend and locally in your browser's extension storage for caching purposes. If you use the "Report" button, we store the domain and your report note to improve future classifications.

Third parties

We do not sell or share collected data with advertisers or third parties. [If you use an AI API like Anthropic/OpenAI for classification, disclose it here — e.g. "Page excerpts may be processed by a third-party AI service to generate a classification."]

Data retention

Cached classification results are retained on our server to avoid reclassifying the same domain repeatedly. You can clear locally cached data by removing the extension.

Your choices

You can stop all data collection at any time by disabling or uninstalling the extension from chrome://extensions.
